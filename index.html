Trained in São Paulo, Miami, and Philadelphia, and in practice in Phoenix for over two decades, Dr. Carpentieri has built a career at the intersection of diagnosis, discovery, and invention.

He completed medical school at the Faculdade de Ciências Médicas da Santa Casa in São Paulo, trained in pediatrics at Jackson Memorial Hospital in Miami, and pursued pathology residency at The Lankenau Hospital before a fellowship in pediatric pathology at The Children's Hospital of Philadelphia. He is board-certified by the American Board of Pathology in both Anatomic & Clinical Pathology and Pediatric Pathology.

Since 2002 he has served as Laboratory Co-Founder and Associate Pathologist at Phoenix Children's, where his roles have spanned laboratory directorship, biorepository medical direction, and section leadership across chemistry, immunology, microbiology, and point-of-care testing. In parallel, he holds academic appointments at the University of Arizona College of Medicine – Phoenix, Mayo Clinic Alix School of Medicine – Scottsdale, and Creighton University Medical Center, and has an intermittent consulting appointment in Dublin, Ireland.

His research has moved from rhabdomyosarcoma and Ewing sarcoma pathology, through pediatric biorepository science and dried-plasma-spot proteomics, to his current focus on the molecular and epigenetic drivers of vascular anomalies — and, through Spectrum, LLC and Ven, LLC, into Raman-spectroscopy-based diagnostic devices.
